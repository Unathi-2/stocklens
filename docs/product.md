# Product definition

## One sentence

StockLens is an interactive analytics platform for exploring JSE and US equities, measuring risk, and experimenting with statistical and machine learning volatility models.

## Target users

Statistics and data science students, analysts, and investors who want analytical evidence rather than recommendations.

## MVP (first release, end of Phase 6)

- Market overview with JSE All Share and S&P 500
- Stock search across JSE and US symbols
- Stock page with profile and interactive price chart
- Log returns, moving averages, annualised volatility

## Differentiators

- JSE coverage next to US markets
- Statistical rigour: tests report statistic, p value and interpretation, never "good" or "bad"
- Volatility focus: forecasts target conditional volatility, evaluated against realised volatility

## Non goals for now

Real trading, broker integration, crypto, options, social features, news.

## User journey

Dashboard, search a symbol, stock page, returns, volatility, statistical tests, model, forecast.
